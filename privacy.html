Privacy Policy for DrawFast

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Matthis Linck, Hettlingshöhe 2, 37627 Deensen, Germany
Email: mlck.mail@proton.me

2. In short

  • No account needed. We don't know your name or email address.
  • Your drawings and photos stay on your iPhone.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, no selling of data.
  • To improve the app, we send pseudonymous usage data to PostHog (servers in the EU). In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland this only happens if you agree. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > "Share usage data".
  • Apple handles purchases. We use RevenueCat to verify them.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Drawings and photos: If you photograph a drawing or pick an image from your photo library, it is stored only inside the app on your iPhone. We never receive it. If you share an image through the iOS share sheet, you decide who gets it.
  • Progress, streak, settings, onboarding answers: stored on your device.
  • Reminders: the daily reminder is a local notification on your iPhone. No data is sent to us for it.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

4. Usage data (PostHog)

What: a random ID created by the app on first launch; which screens, lessons and drawings you open and finish; steps in onboarding and on the purchase screen; whether you have Premium; app version, device type, iOS version, language and country (from your App Store country and your IP address, which is not stored permanently). If the app crashes, a technical crash report (device, iOS version, where in the program the error happened) may be sent. We don't record your screen.

Why: to understand where people get stuck and to improve the lessons and the app.

Legal basis: in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). In other countries, our legitimate interest in a working and better app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object at any time by turning the switch off.

Who: PostHog Inc., 2261 Market St., #4008, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA. Data is stored on servers in the EU (Frankfurt). PostHog processes it on our behalf. Any access from the US is covered by EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long: usage events and crash reports at most 12 months.

5. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions and purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple's privacy policy applies to them.

So that the app recognises your purchases and we can analyse sales, we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives the random app ID, Apple's purchase receipts (product, date, price, currency, country, trial, cancellation or renewal) and device data such as model, iOS version and IP address. Apple reports subscription changes directly to RevenueCat. RevenueCat forwards purchase events (without payment details) to our PostHog account.

Legal basis: performing the contract for your purchase (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in analysing our sales (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who: RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA, as our processor. Transfers to the US are covered by EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long: as long as you use the app and legal retention duties apply.

6. What we don't do

No ads, no ad tracking, no access to the advertising identifier (IDFA), no selling or sharing of data, no disclosure to third parties other than the service providers named above.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us at mlck.mail@proton.me. Because we don't know you by name, it helps if you tell us roughly when you used the app. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have similar rights; we do not sell or share personal information.

You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

8. Children

DrawFast is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children.

9. Changes

If the app changes, we update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
