FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

In DrawFast open Settings and tap "Restore purchases" (or "Restore" on the Premium screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In DrawFast tap Settings > "Manage subscription", or on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose DrawFast and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I turn off usage data?

In DrawFast open Settings and turn off "Share usage data".
